Delete your HopOn account

HopOn is an app by HopOn Studio. This page explains how to delete your HopOn account and the information that goes with it, with or without the app.

In the app

  1. Open HopOn and tap the gear to open Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Type the confirmation phrase, enter your password, and hold the button.

Your account is deleted immediately. It cannot be undone.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, or cannot sign in, email us from the email address of your account:

Email hoponstudios@gmail.com

Use the subject Delete my account. We will confirm that the request comes from the account holder and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your email address, password and all sign-in sessions.
  • Your username, display name, photo, mascot, skin and colour.
  • Your push-notification tokens and notification settings.
  • Your aura and its whole history.
  • Every item you own, including items you paid for with real money.
  • Trials in which you were the accused, your sentences, and the reactions thrown at you.
  • The hours you offered for squad-picked times, and your nudges.
  • Your membership of every squad.

What is kept

Sessions and trials are shared with other people, so a few records stay, attached to an empty placeholder called “Deleted User”. They no longer contain your name, username, photo or email address.

  • Your place in past sessions: your answer, whether and when you joined, and whether you were late, left early or did not show up. Kept for as long as the squad it belongs to exists.
  • Your part in other people’s closed trials, reactions you threw at others, and trials you filed against others, with no name on them. Kept for as long as the squad exists.
  • Records of real-money purchases, with the account identifier they were made under. Kept for as long as needed for accounting, refunds, disputes and fraud prevention, and for as long as the law requires.
  • Backups and server logs can hold deleted information for a limited period before they are overwritten.

Text that other people typed is not rewritten. If someone wrote your name in a field of their own, email us and we will review it.

More

Uninstalling the app or signing out does not delete your account. The full details are in section 11 of the Privacy Policy.
