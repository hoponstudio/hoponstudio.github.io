HopOn Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026
Last updated: 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information HopOn collects, why it is collected, who can see it, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and the choices and rights you have.

It applies to the HopOn mobile application, the servers behind it, and the emails sent to operate it (together, "HopOn" or "the service").

Please read it before you use HopOn. By creating an account or using HopOn you confirm that you have read this policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use HopOn.

The short version

  • HopOn is a social app for friends who play games together: squads, summons to play, live sessions, a player card, an in-app score called aura, and a mock "squad court".
  • We collect what the app needs to work: your email address, the profile you create, what you do in squads and sessions, a push-notification token, and basic technical information such as your IP address.
  • Much of what you do in HopOn is visible to the people in your squads. That is the purpose of the app. Section 5 says exactly who can see what.
  • We do not sell your information. HopOn has no advertising, no advertising identifiers, and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • HopOn does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone or camera.
  • Your information is stored in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Some of the service providers we rely on are outside the EU.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time. Deletion is immediate and permanent. Some records of sessions you shared with other people remain, without your name. Section 11 explains this.
  • HopOn is for people aged 16 and over.
  • Questions and requests: hoponstudios@gmail.com

This summary is only a guide. The full policy below is what applies.

HopOn is operated by Mohamed Imad AlSatary, an individual developer from Jordan, who publishes the app under the name HopOn Studio.

HopOn Studio is a brand name. It is not a separately incorporated company.

In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean the operator of HopOn. Where data-protection law uses the term, we are the "controller" of the personal information described here.

Contact: hoponstudios@gmail.com

We have not appointed a data protection officer. Privacy questions and requests go to the address above.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers information handled by HopOn itself.

It does not cover:

  • the games you play, or the voice and chat services you use alongside HopOn;
  • the services of other companies mentioned in this policy, which handle information under their own privacy policies; or
  • what other people do with information they have seen in HopOn, including screenshots and anything shared outside the app.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Account information

  • Email address. Used to create your account, sign you in, send verification codes and reset your password. Your email address is never shown to other users.
  • Password. Stored by our authentication provider only as a one-way cryptographic hash. We cannot read your password.
  • One-time codes. The 6-digit codes we email you are kept only for a short time, until they are used or expire.
  • Account identifiers and dates. A unique user ID, the date your account was created and the time you last signed in.

3.2 Profile information

  • Username. Chosen at sign-up. It cannot be changed afterwards.
  • Display name.
  • Mascot, skin and mascot colour you have chosen or equipped.
  • Photo (optional). If you add a photo to your player card, we store it. You pick it with your phone's own photo picker, so HopOn can only see the photo you select, not the rest of your gallery. Before it is uploaded, the photo is reduced in size on your phone and the technical details your camera embedded in the file, such as the device model, the date the photo was taken and any location, are removed. Only the picture itself is sent.

3.3 Squad information

  • The squads you create or join, their names and join codes.
  • Who created each squad, your role in it and the date you joined.
  • A short-lived record of failed attempts to enter a join code, used to limit repeated guessing.

3.4 Summons and gaming sessions

  • Summons you send: the squad, who was invited, the game, the time or the candidate days, any custom wording for the reply buttons, any note you add, and later edits or cancellations.
  • Your answers to summons (in, maybe, out) and when you gave them.
  • The hours you say you can play when a squad is choosing a time, and your votes on a time.
  • Nudges you send and receive.
  • Attendance: whether and when you joined a session, whether you were on time or late, whether you left early, and whether you did not show up after saying you would.
  • Markers recording which reminders and notifications have been sent.

3.5 Live sessions

While a live session runs we store the state needed to run it and a timeline of what happened, including:

  • who started it, who is captain and when that role passed to someone else;
  • who is in the room, including "still here" signals your app sends about once a minute while the live room is open on your screen;
  • game picks, wheel spins and their results;
  • race runners, results and aura bets;
  • polls, votes and their results;
  • breaks, mutes and removals from the room;
  • GIFs or text a captain puts on the room's screen; and
  • when the session started and ended, including if it closed by itself because nobody was there.

Lines you type, reactions and thrown items in a live room are relayed in real time to the other people in that room and are not saved in our database. Other people in the room will see them, and their phones may read typed lines out loud using the phone's own text-to-speech engine.

3.6 Aura, statistics and activity

  • Aura ledger. Every amount of aura you earn, lose, bet or spend, the reason, the related squad, session or item, and the time.
  • Player card statistics and rating. Calculated from your session history, such as how often you answer summons, say yes, show up, arrive on time and stay to the end.
  • Activity summaries. Calculated when requested from your session records: number of sessions, time spent in live sessions, the days and hours of the week you play, your most played games, and summons you sent or hosted. To work these out by your local day, your app sends your device's time-zone offset with the request. It is not stored.

3.7 Squad court, sentences and reactions

HopOn includes a mock court as a game between friends. We store:

  • Trials. The accused, the squad and session concerned, how the trial started (opened automatically when someone did not show up after saying they would, started during a live session, or filed by a squadmate), the written charge, any plea, the judge, the jurors, each ballot, the verdict, and the relevant times.
  • Sentences. The kind of sentence (for example a changed display name, a changed look or colour, a title, a stamp, a race handicap, a betting ban or an aura fine), its details, whether it applies in one squad or everywhere, and when it starts and ends.
  • Reactions. What squadmates throw at a sentenced member, by whom, how many and when.
  • Record. Past verdicts and sentences.

3.8 Shop and virtual items

  • The items you own, whether each was obtained with aura or with money, the aura price paid and the date.
  • Which item you have equipped.

3.9 Purchases

If you buy something with real money, we receive and store a record of the transaction: the store, whether it was a real or a test purchase, the product, the transaction identifier, the price and currency, your HopOn account ID, whether it was a purchase or a refund, the outcome and the time.

We never receive or store your card number or other full payment details. Payment is handled by the app store.

3.10 Device, notification and technical information

  • Push-notification token, the platform it belongs to (for example Android), the account it is linked to and when it was last updated.
  • Notification settings: which kinds of notification you want, in general and for each squad, and which squads you have muted.
  • IP address and user agent. Our authentication provider records your IP address and a technical description of the software making the request each time a sign-in session is created or refreshed. Our hosting provider also keeps short-lived server logs that include IP addresses and the requests made.
  • Server diagnostics. Error and activity logs from our servers, which can include account IDs.

3.11 Information stored on your device

HopOn keeps the following on your phone:

  • a sign-in token, so you stay signed in;
  • your settings for haptics, reduced motion and voices;
  • copies of information the server has sent (for example your squads, cards, sessions and the shop), so the app can open quickly and without a connection. This store is limited to a few megabytes, entries are removed after about 30 days without use, and all of it is erased when you sign out or another account signs in; and
  • cached pictures, such as squadmates' photos and game cover art.

This information is removed when you uninstall the app. HopOn does not allow it to be included in your phone's cloud backup or copied across to a new phone, so on a new phone you sign in again.

3.12 Messages you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

3.13 Reports, blocks and the terms of use

  • Reports. When you report a person, a squad or a trial, we store who made the report (you), the reason you picked, any note you wrote, the time, and a copy of what was reported as it was at that moment: for a person, their username, display name, photo and mascot; for a squad, its name, who created it and how many members it has; for a trial, the charge, the plea, the names of the accused and the accuser, and the ballots. When you report someone from a live room, the last few lines they typed, as your phone received them, are included. The person or squad you report is not told, and is never told who reported them.
  • Blocks. Who you blocked, and when. The person you block is not told and cannot see it.
  • The terms of use. Which version of the Terms of Use you agreed to, and when.

3.14 What HopOn does not collect

HopOn does not collect:

  • your precise or approximate location from your device;
  • your contacts, calendar, call logs or text messages;
  • audio from your microphone, or images from your camera;
  • advertising identifiers;
  • your card number or bank details; or
  • analytics, usage tracking or crash reports through tools such as Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics or Sentry.

HopOn does not use cookies and does not track you across other apps or websites.

If any of this changes, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.

4. Content created by you, and about you

4.1 Content you create

HopOn lets you type and upload content, including your display name, squad names, the note and reply wording on a summon, poll questions, text for a room's screen, lines typed in a live room, charges and pleas in the squad court, and your photo.

You are responsible for what you submit. Please:

  • do not include sensitive personal information about yourself or anyone else, such as health details, home addresses, identity numbers or financial information;
  • only upload a photo you have the right to use, and do not upload a photo of another person without their permission; and
  • remember that what you submit is shown to other people and can be copied by them.

4.2 Content other people create about you

The squad court is part of the game. Your squadmates can file a charge against you, write a reason for it and vote on it, and a jury can sentence you to in-game effects such as a changed display name, a changed look or a title, for a limited time.

Charges, reasons, titles and names written by other users are their own words. They are not statements by us, we do not check them for accuracy, and we do not review content before it appears.

If something written about you in HopOn is abusive, or reveals personal information about you, report it from inside the app or email hoponstudios@gmail.com, and we will review it.

4.3 Reporting, blocking and moderation

Everyone who uses HopOn agrees to the Terms of Use, which say what is not allowed. You can report a person, a squad or a trial from inside the app, and block anyone. Reports come to us; section 3.13 says what a report contains. Blocking someone stops their lines, photo, notifications and court actions from reaching you, and they are not told.

We review reports. We do not monitor everything users submit and are under no obligation to do so. We may remove content, or suspend or delete accounts, where we believe the service is being misused, these terms are broken, or the law requires it.

7. Automated processing

HopOn works things out automatically as part of the game. It calculates your statistics, rating and aura from your session history, opens a trial automatically when someone does not show up after saying they would, and casts a random ballot for a juror who does not vote in some kinds of trial.

These outcomes affect only your standing and appearance inside HopOn. We do not make decisions about you by automated means that have legal effects or similarly significant effects.

11. Deleting your account and your information

11.1 In the app

Go to Settings → Account → Delete account. You will be asked to type a confirmation phrase, enter your password and hold a button.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. There is no waiting period and no recovery.

11.2 Without the app

If you cannot use the app, you can ask us to delete your account:

  • by email to hoponstudios@gmail.com, sent from the email address of the account, with the subject "Delete my account"; or
  • through our deletion request page: https://hoponstudio.github.io/delete-account/

We will confirm that the request comes from the account holder and delete the account within 30 days.

11.3 What is deleted

  • your email address, password and all sign-in sessions;
  • your username, display name, photo (including the stored file), mascot, skin and colour. Your username becomes available for anyone to register again;
  • your push tokens and notification settings;
  • your aura and its whole ledger;
  • every item you own, including items you paid for with real money;
  • trials in which you were the accused, your sentences, and the reactions thrown at you;
  • the hours you offered for squad-picked times, and your nudges;
  • the people you blocked, and anyone's block of you, and the record of which terms you agreed to; and
  • your membership of every squad.

11.4 What happens to things you were running

  • Squads you created pass to the member who has been in the squad longest. A squad with no other member is deleted, together with its history.
  • A live session you are captain of passes to another participant, or ends if you are alone in it.
  • A summon of yours that has not started is called off, and the people you summoned are told.
  • An open trial where you are a juror continues without your ballot.

11.5 What is kept, without your name

Sessions and trials are shared with other people, and removing your part entirely would damage their history. So the following stays, attached to an empty placeholder called "Deleted User" with a generic avatar:

  • your place in past sessions, including sessions you summoned or captained: your answer, whether and when you joined, and whether you were late, left early or did not show up;
  • your ballots and other parts in other people's closed trials;
  • reactions you threw at other people;
  • trials you filed against other people, with the accuser shown as "Deleted User";
  • reports you made, without your name on them, until they expire (section 10); and
  • the internal identifier of the placeholder.

These records no longer contain your name, username, photo or email address, and we no longer link them to you. People who played with you may still recognise which place was yours.

Text that other people typed is not rewritten. If a squadmate wrote your name in a charge, a title or another free-text field of their own, it stays as they wrote it. Contact us if you want us to review something specific.

Reports other people made about you are kept as they were made, including the copy of what was reported, which can include your username, display name and photo, until they expire one year after we close them (section 10). We keep them to deal with misuse of HopOn and to protect the people who use it.

11.6 Purchase records

Records of real-money purchases are kept after deletion, together with the account identifier they were made under, for accounting, refunds, disputes, fraud prevention and legal compliance. Keeping these records does not mean your account still exists.

11.7 Information held by other companies

  • The app store and RevenueCat keep their own records of your purchases. These are not deleted automatically when you delete your HopOn account. You can ask us to request deletion of the record RevenueCat holds for you, subject to what must be kept by law.
  • Google may retain technical records relating to push notifications under its own policies.
  • Content that you or others shared outside HopOn is beyond our control.

11.8 Other things to know

  • Uninstalling the app or signing out does not delete your account.
  • Deleted information may remain in backups and server logs for a limited period before it is overwritten.
  • After deletion you may sign up again with the same email address. It will be a new account: nothing from the old one is restored.
  • You can change your display name, replace your photo, change your mascot colour, change your notification settings and leave a squad at any time without deleting your account. To have your photo removed without replacing it, or to correct your email address, email us.

12. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between the app and our servers;
  • encryption of stored data by our hosting provider;
  • passwords stored only as one-way hashes;
  • access rules enforced on the server, so that a user can read only what the app is meant to show them, in general their own information and that of people in their squads;
  • profile photos stored privately, and handed only to their owner and to members of their squads;
  • actions that matter, such as earning aura, verdicts, purchases and account deletion, being decided on the server rather than on a phone;
  • secret keys kept on the server and out of the app; and
  • limits on file types and sizes, and on repeated attempts.

No app, server or method of transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of your information. You use HopOn knowing this.

You can help: use a strong password that you do not use elsewhere, keep your phone locked, and do not share your password. If you believe your account has been accessed without your permission, email us straight away.

If we become aware of a security breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities where the law requires it.

13. Children and age requirements

HopOn is intended for people who are at least 16 years old. Anyone under 16 is not permitted to create or use a HopOn account.

If you are 16 or older but under the age at which you are legally an adult where you live, you may use HopOn only with the permission of a parent or legal guardian, who should read this policy with you.

HopOn does not verify age automatically. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 16, we will delete the account and the information associated with it, apart from any limited information we are required or permitted to keep.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, email hoponstudios@gmail.com.
