Get HopOn early

HopOn is in closed beta on Android. Three taps and you're in. Then bring your squad: it's made for the friends you play with.

  1. Join the beta group

    Sign in with the Google account your phone's Play Store uses, then press Join group.

    Join the group

  2. Become a tester

    Press Become a tester. If it says you're not in the group yet, give it a minute after step 1 and open it again.

    Become a tester

  3. Install HopOn

    It's on Google Play now, for you. Install it, sign up, make a squad, summon your friends.

    Get it on Google Play

On iPhone? Not yet. It's on the way.
