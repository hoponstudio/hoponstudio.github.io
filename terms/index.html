6. Reporting and blocking

Reporting. You can report:

  • a person, from the ⋯ on their card, from their name on the Crew page, or by tapping them in a live room;
  • a squad, from the ⋯ menu on the squad's page; and
  • a trial, from the flag at the top of the trial.

A report goes to us, with a copy of what was reported. When you report someone from a live room, the last few lines they typed, as your phone received them, go with the report. The person or squad you report is not told, and is never told who reported them.

We review reports and act on them when we find that these terms were broken. We may not be able to tell you what we did. Please report honestly: using reports to harass someone breaks these terms too.

Blocking. You can block anyone from the same places, and unblock them in Settings → Account → Blocked. They are not told. Once you block someone:

  • their lines in a live room are not shown or read out on your phone, and their tomatoes and reactions do not reach you;
  • their photo is not shown to you;
  • their summons, nudges and answers do not send you notifications; and
  • they cannot put you on trial, vote in your trials, or throw things at you while you serve a sentence.

You stay in the squads you share with them. You can leave a squad at any time.
